# Dreadless

Dreadless turns an admin job you keep putting off into tiny steps, and shows you only the next one.

This page holds the **test builds** of the Android app. The app's code isn't here, only the installable files.

## Install

1. Open the [latest release](https://github.com/dineshsirkar/dreadless-releases/releases/latest) on your Android phone (Android 8 or newer).
2. Under **Assets**, tap the `.apk` file to download it, then open it.
3. Allow your browser to install apps when Android asks.
4. If Google Play Protect warns that the app is from an unknown developer, tap **Install anyway**. That warning appears because the app isn't on the Play Store yet.

On some Samsung phones, **Auto Blocker** (Settings → Security and privacy) stops apps from outside the Play Store. Switch it off while you install, then back on.

## Updates

In the app, open **Settings → Check for updates**. When a newer version is here, the app downloads it and Android asks you to confirm the update. Your jobs stay on your phone through updates.

## Your data

Your jobs are stored only on your own phone. Dreadless has no account and no server, and it never sends your jobs anywhere. It connects to the internet only when you tap **Check for updates**.
