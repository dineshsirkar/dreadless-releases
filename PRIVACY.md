# Dreadless privacy policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026

Dreadless is built to keep your information on your own phone.

## What Dreadless stores
- The jobs, steps and notes you type in, your progress (XP, streak, town, rewards) and your settings.
- In family mode: your kids' first names, their chores, the amount set for each chore and each kid's running allowance total. The parent PIN is stored only as a scrambled code, never as the digits. Dreadless never moves money.
- Chore photos: taken with the phone's camera app and kept inside Dreadless's own private storage, not the gallery. A parent can choose to save one to the gallery, and can turn on deleting photos after approval.
- These are saved only on your phone, in the app's private storage, plus a backup copy in your phone's Downloads/Dreadless folder so you can restore them if the app is ever removed.

## What Dreadless does not do
- No account and no sign-in.
- No ads, no analytics and no tracking.
- Your jobs are never sent to us or to anyone else.

## Permissions and why
- **Notifications:** reminders, the 5-minute timer alert and the optional daily nudge.
- **Calendar (only if you turn it on):** puts one event per job with a reminder into a calendar your phone already syncs. Dreadless only adds, updates and removes its own events.
- **Reading your calendar (only if you turn on "#do" events):** reads event titles and times on your phone to turn events tagged #do into jobs, and to show upcoming events you can turn into a job with one tap. Nothing is copied off the phone, and Dreadless never changes or deletes those events. Some company work profiles block apps from reading the calendar; then this feature can't work on that phone.
- **Internet and install updates (test builds only):** checks this GitHub page for a newer version and installs it when you tap Install. Nothing about you is sent; it only downloads the version file and the app.
- **Vibration:** a short buzz when you clear a step.
- **Nearby devices, Bluetooth and Wi-Fi (family mode only):** lets the parent's phone and a paired kid's phone swap chores, photos and allowance totals directly, using Google's Nearby Connections, with no internet and no server. Every message is signed with a key only your family's phones hold. It runs only while Dreadless is open. On Android 12 and older, Android calls this permission "location", because Bluetooth scanning needs it there; Dreadless never reads or stores your location.
- **QR code scanning (family mode, kid's phone):** uses Google's code scanner from Google Play services, so Dreadless itself doesn't get camera access for it.
- **Camera (family mode, kid's phone):** Dreadless opens your phone's own camera app to take a chore photo. It doesn't ask for camera permission itself.
- **Start at boot:** puts your reminders back after the phone restarts or the app updates.

## Sharing text into Dreadless
When you share text from another app into Dreadless, it only fills in the new job form on your phone.

## Deleting your data
Uninstall the app, then delete the Downloads/Dreadless folder.
